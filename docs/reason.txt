Reasoning for:
outputs/embedding_svd_error/plots/display/svd_error_targets_compression.png

Why the outliers formed:

The outliers are mostly not caused by unusually large segments.
Most high-compression outlier rows still have normal segment size:
n_frames = 149 and matrix_rank = 149.

They formed because some segment matrices need a much smaller selected_k
to satisfy the same target error epsilon.

Compression ratio is:

original_params / svd_params

and svd_params depends directly on k:

svd_params = k * (N + d_model + 1)

So when selected_k is smaller, compression_ratio becomes much larger.

What this means mathematically:

For those outlier segments, the embedding matrix energy is concentrated in
fewer singular directions. A low-rank reconstruction captures most of the
matrix with fewer SVD components.

Panel-wise reason:

1. embedding_frames

This has the strongest outliers.
Some normal-sized segments reach epsilon = 0.10 with only k = 4.
That is why their compression ratio goes very high.

Reason: raw frame embeddings inside those segments are highly similar /
low-rank in embedding space.

2. embedding_delta_previous_i

This has moderate outliers.
The clearest repeated case is display_000102.
It reaches target errors with much smaller k than typical segments.

Reason: deltas from previous I-frame are unusually structured for that
segment, so fewer SVD components explain most of the delta matrix.

3. embedding_delta_adjacent

This panel is tighter.
Even its outliers still need fairly large k, around 89 to 91 for epsilon = 0.10.

Reason: adjacent-frame delta matrices are less low-rank. The frame-to-frame
changes are more varied, so compression is weaker and outliers are mild.

Tiny-segment near-zero points:

Some near-zero-error points come from very short segments, e.g. n_frames = 3.
When k reaches the full possible rank of such a tiny matrix, reconstruction
becomes almost exact.

These are not meaningful high-compression outliers because their compression
ratio is about 1.

Faithful conclusion:

The main outliers happen because selected_k is unusually small, not because
the segments are larger. Smaller selected_k means fewer SVD parameters, which
creates a larger compression ratio. This indicates those segments are more
low-rank / redundant in the chosen embedding representation.
